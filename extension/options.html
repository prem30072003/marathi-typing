स्वागत आहे! Welcome

Click into any text box on any website and type Marathi the way you chat: tumhi kase aahat → तुम्ही कसे आहात. Press Space to accept, 1–7 to pick another word, Esc to keep English, and Alt+M to switch on/off. Try it right here:
